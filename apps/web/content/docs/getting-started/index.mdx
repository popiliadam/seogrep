Getting started takes three steps: get your personal MCP URL, add it to your AI
client, and ask for an analysis in plain language.

1. **Get your MCP URL.** From your SeoGrep dashboard, copy your personal MCP
   endpoint. In these docs it appears as `YOUR_MCP_URL`.
2. **Add it to your client.** Paste `YOUR_MCP_URL` into the MCP server settings
   of the client you already use — Claude Desktop, claude.ai, Claude Code,
   Cursor, or Windsurf.
3. **Ask in plain language.** Try "Crawl my site and find quick wins." Credits
   pay for the analysis work; your AI subscription stays your own.

## Two ways to connect

- **Your personal URL (default).** Paste `YOUR_MCP_URL` — the whole URL,
  including your key — into your client's MCP server settings. This works in
  every client and is what the dashboard gives you.
- **Fixed URL plus a header.** Point the client at `https://mcp.seogrep.com/mcp`
  and send your key in an `x-api-key` header (`Authorization: Bearer YOUR_KEY`
  also works). Some directories and proxies only support this form, and it keeps
  your key out of URLs, which get written to logs and browser history.

Both accept the same key and expose the same tools — use whichever your client
supports.

> **One-time URL:** your MCP URL is shown only once, right when a key is
> created or rotated — the dashboard shows just a masked prefix after
> that. Save it somewhere safe. If you lose it, rotate your key from the
> Connection page to get a fresh URL (the old one stops working
> immediately).

> **Beta note:** SeoGrep is in private beta. Setup details and the exact tool
> list will be finalized at launch.
