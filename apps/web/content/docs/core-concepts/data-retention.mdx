SeoGrep keeps different data for different lengths of time. To close your
account and have your data removed, contact us at support@seogrep.com and we will handle it for
you — with one exception, the append-only credit ledger, described below.

> **Beta note:** Retention windows below are the current beta policy and may
> be refined before launch.

## What's kept, and for how long

- **Crawl raw data** — during beta, retained while your account is active.
- **Search Console data** — during beta, retained while your account is
  active.
- **Report output** — kept for the lifetime of your account, so you can
  reference or reshare it later.
- **Credit ledger entries** — the record of what you bought and what you
  spent. The ledger is append-only by design, so these entries are never
  deleted; see below.

## Deleting your data

To have your data deleted, email us at support@seogrep.com and we will remove it. There's no
self-serve delete option in beta yet, so reach out and we'll handle it for
you.

### What is not deleted

The credit ledger is the exception. It is append-only by design, so the
entries showing what you bought and what you spent are kept as accounting
records rather than erased. Those entries are tied to your account record,
so that record is kept alongside them. Everything else above — crawl raw
data, Search Console data and the stored token, and report output together
with its share links — is removed.

The [Privacy Policy](/privacy) states the same exception under Your rights,
alongside the GDPR and KVKK rights it does not affect.
