Short answers to the questions that come up most before and during setup.

> **Beta note:** Answers below reflect the current beta.

## What is MCP?

The Model Context Protocol is an open standard that lets AI clients call
tools on a remote server. SeoGrep is an MCP server — your client calls its
tools directly, inside your own chat.

## Which clients work?

Claude Desktop, claude.ai, Claude Code, Cursor, and Windsurf. See
[Getting started](/docs/getting-started) for setup steps per client.

## Do I need Google Search Console?

No. Your first crawl and audit run without it. Connecting GSC unlocks
performance-based tools like decay analysis — see
[GSC connection](/docs/core-concepts/gsc-connection).

## What do credits cost?

See [/pricing](/pricing) for the current credit cost of each run.

## Is my data used to train AI models?

No. Your site data is never used to train AI models — see
[Security](/docs/security).

## Do I need an invite?

No. Sign up on the homepage and your account is ready as soon as you confirm
your email address. You start with 200 free credits and no card is required.

## How do I delete my data?

Email support@seogrep.com and ask for a deletion — we'll
remove it. One exception: the credit ledger is append-only, so the entries
showing what you bought and what you spent are kept as accounting records.
See [Data retention](/docs/core-concepts/data-retention).
