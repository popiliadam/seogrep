SeoGrep runs on monthly credit plans with optional top-ups — see
[/pricing](/pricing) for the current plans and credit costs. This page
covers billing mechanics, not the prices themselves.

> **Beta note:** Checkout is live — plans and top-ups can be bought today.
> New accounts still start on trial credits.

## Plans and top-ups

Paid plans renew your credit balance every month. If you run out mid-month,
a top-up adds credits without changing your plan. During beta, unused
credits stay in your balance and don't expire.

## What trial credits cover

Trial credits run the crawler, the audits that read what the crawl already
stored (`audit_onpage`, `audit_tech`, `audit_schema`, `audit_content`),
reports, the quick-win finders, the Search Console tools, and the two free
halves of the rank tracker (`track_keywords` and `keyword_positions`).

The exception is every tool that spends money with a third-party data
provider on your behalf: the keyword research and gap tools, the SERP
snapshot, the backlink tools, the AI-visibility tools, and `audit_speed` —
which despite the family name buys a page-speed run per URL rather than
reading stored measurements. Those need a **paid credit balance**. On a
trial account they refuse *before* anything is reserved, so the call spends
no credits and writes no ledger entry at all. Any purchase — a plan or a
top-up — unlocks them, and your trial credits are untouched and stay
spendable on everything else.

Each tool's own page in the [Tools Reference](/docs/tools-reference) states
whether it needs a paid balance; that is the list to trust, because it is
generated from the same code the gate runs.

## Payment provider

Payments are handled by Paddle, our merchant of record, so receipts, tax,
and card handling go through their checkout — SeoGrep never stores your
card details.

## Checking usage

Ask "What's my credit balance?" to run `get_credit_balance` any time, or
open the dashboard's Usage tab for a full ledger of what each run cost.

## Changing or cancelling

Plan changes and cancellations happen from the dashboard's Billing tab,
which opens Paddle's billing portal.
